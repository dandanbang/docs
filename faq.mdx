Our philosophy is to build the best possible experience for our subscribers and, at the same time, share the benefits of Life Note, journaling, and AI mentors with as many people as possible.

Life Note is supported entirely by subscription. We will never sell your data.

## What you get

|  | Free | Subscription |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Journaling | 3 entries per week | Unlimited |
| Mentor responses | 1 per entry | Up to 3 per entry |
| Deep Chat | Limited usage | More usage, deeper conversations |
| Memory quality | Medium | High |
| Voice journaling | 10 recordings per week | 200 per month |
| Photos in entries | Standard resolution | Full resolution |
| Scan Journal | Weekly limit | Higher limit |
| Life Goals | Basic mentor guidance | Deeper mentor integration |

<Tip>
  **Your first week is wider.** New accounts get 10 entries in their first seven days rather than 3, so you can find your rhythm before the weekly pace settles in.
</Tip>
