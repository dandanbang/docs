Everything else in Life Note shows you your life as a list. This shows it as a place.

Your entries become glowing orbs strung along a path through a valley. Walk it, and you are walking your own timeline — the further you go, the further back or forward you travel. The weather changes as you move, because it is made from how you actually felt during the stretch you are standing in.

<Info>
  Your Inner World opens once you have **15 entries**, and runs on desktop only — it needs a bigger screen and a real keyboard to walk around in.
</Info>

## Walking it

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Move" icon="keyboard">
    **W A S D** to walk, or simply click anywhere on the ground. Hold **Shift** to run.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Rise" icon="feather">
    **Space** to jump. Hold it to lift off and fly, then glide back down.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Touch a memory" icon="hand-sparkles">
    Walk up to an orb and press **E** to open that day and read it.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Let go" icon="person-walking">
    Press **F** and the spirit strolls the path on its own while you watch your life go by.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

## What the light means

- **Glowing orbs** are your memories. Their colour is the emotion they carry — joy, calm, sadness, anger, fear, love, and the rest each have their own.
- **Golden lights** off the path are pieces of wisdom you saved to your collection.
- **Glowing frames** are your Inner Portraits, standing in the year they were made.

The best things are not on the path. Wander sideways.

## Moving through years

Use the year list on the right to jump, or the **[** and **]** keys to step back and forward a year at a time.

## The weather is yours

The sky is not decoration. It reads the dominant emotion of the memories around you and turns radiant, clear, misty, rainy, or stormy to match. Walk through a hard winter and you will feel it change.

<Tip>
  Press **?** at any time inside the valley to bring the controls back up.
</Tip>
