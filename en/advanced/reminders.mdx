Journaling works best as a regular practice. But life gets busy. Days slip by. That's where reminders come in—not as nagging, but as gentle invitations to pause and reflect.

On your phone you can set up to 10 reminders, each tailored to your schedule. Life Note can also tell you when your Sunday Letter is ready, or check in when you have gone quiet.

<Info>
  **Where to find it:** on your phone, Settings → **Manage Notifications**. On the web, Settings → **Notifications**.
</Info>

## Setting up journal reminders

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="Mobile">
    <Steps>
      <Step title="Open Notifications">
        Go to Settings → Manage Notifications
      </Step>
      <Step title="Tap Add Reminder">
        You'll see a form to create your reminder
      </Step>
      <Step title="Choose your time">
        Select the time of day that works best for you
      </Step>
      <Step title="Select days">
        Pick which days of the week this reminder should appear. You can choose specific days or every day.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Save">
        Your reminder is now active
      </Step>
    </Steps>
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Web">
    Go to Settings → Notifications and turn on **Journal reminders**. Choose **Every day**, **Weekdays** or **Custom**, then pick a time.

    If you use the app, the reminder arrives on your phone, and reminders you made there show up under **Also set on your phone**. If you don't, it comes by email.
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

A reminder usually brings a question from your main mentor. On Life Note 1.0.116 or later, a notification from a mentor shows that mentor's face.

### Also by email

Turn on **Also by email** to get an email as well as the phone notification. Find it when you add or edit a reminder on your phone, or under Journal reminders on the web.

## Managing your reminders

Each reminder you create appears under **Journal reminders**. From here you can:

- **Toggle on/off** — Temporarily disable a reminder without deleting it
- **Edit** — Change the time or days
- **Delete** — Remove the reminder entirely

You can have up to 10 reminders active at once. Maybe one for morning reflection and another for evening review. Maybe different reminders for weekdays versus weekends.

---

## From Life Note

Below your reminders, three switches decide what else Life Note sends you.

- **Sunday Letter** — when your letter and Inner Portrait are ready. A notification, or an email if you don't use the app.
- **Mentor check-ins** — when you've been quiet on a practice, a quest or your journal.
- **Tips & milestones** — welcome tips and milestones.

Receipts, rewards and account emails always arrive, whatever you choose here.

---

## Privacy

On your phone, turn on **Hide content in notifications** to show a general alert instead of mentor messages or details from your journal. Useful if other people can see your lock screen.

---

## Notification permissions

For reminders to work on your phone, Life Note needs permission to send you notifications.

If you haven't granted permission yet, Notifications shows a note at the top with a way to fix it. If you turned notifications off earlier, enable them for Life Note in your phone's settings.

<Note>
  On iOS, you'll need to allow notifications in your device Settings. On Android, you may be prompted directly within the app.
</Note>

## Finding the right rhythm

There's no universal "best time" to journal. Some people find mornings work well—capturing thoughts before the day takes over. Others prefer evenings—processing what happened and letting go before sleep.

Experiment. Start with one reminder at a time that feels natural. Add more only if it helps, not to feel pressured.

The goal isn't to journal every time a reminder appears. It's to keep the invitation open—so that when you're ready to write, you remember that you can.
