Settings now has fewer, clearer sections. Everything about your mentors sits in one place, and the things you rarely touch sit at the bottom.

## On your phone

Tap the profile icon, then **Settings**.

| Section | What's inside |
|---|---|
| **Profile** | Your name and photo. **People** appears here once Life Note has found someone you write about. |
| **Mentors** | Main Mentor, Default journal responders, Guidance Mode, **Tell your mentors about you**, and **Manage Blocked Mentors**. |
| **Journal Reminder & Notification** | **Manage Notifications** opens your reminders and everything else Life Note can send you. |
| **Share Life Note** and **Gift Life Note** | Your referral link and how many friends have joined, plus a gift for someone else. |
| **Support** | **Send Feedback** goes straight to a person. |
| **Security** | Face ID or fingerprint unlock, on phones that have it. |
| **Appearance & language** | System, Light or Dark, your **Language**, and the **App Icon**. |
| **Community** | Our Discord and Instagram. |
| **Legal** | Terms and Conditions, and the Privacy Policy. |
| **Account** | Billing, **Import & export**, **Log out** and **Account Management**. |

<Note>
  This layout comes with Life Note 1.0.116. If your Settings looks different, update the app from the App Store or Google Play.
</Note>

### Your subscription

If you subscribe through your phone, **Billing** shows your plan. **Manage subscription** takes you to the App Store or Google Play, where you can change or cancel it. If you pay monthly, **Switch to yearly billing** appears under Billing.

### Import & export

Importing and exporting happen on the web, where there is room for a file. **Import & export** on your phone gives you the address: **Copy link**, or **Send to my computer** to send it to yourself from your phone's share sheet. See [Bring your history in](/en/start-here/bring-your-history-in) and [Privacy and security](/en/start-here/privacy-and-security).

### Log out

**Log out** now asks once before it signs you out, and shows the email you can sign back in with.

## Choose your app icon

Go to **Settings → Appearance & language → App Icon** and pick one of eight. **Night** is the default. The others are Morning sky, Forest, Midnight, Dusk, Nap, Spellbook and Classic. Your phone may show a short confirmation when the icon changes.

## On the web

Click your name at the bottom of the sidebar, then **Settings**. The same menu has **Guide**, **Share Life Note**, **Join Discord** and **Logout**.

| Section | What's inside |
|---|---|
| **Profile Information** | Your photo and name. |
| **Preferences** | **Language** first, then Main Mentor, Default journal responders, Guidance Mode, About You and Blocked Mentors. |
| **Notifications** | Journal reminders, Sunday Letter, Mentor check-ins, and Tips & milestones. See [Reminders & Notifications](/en/advanced/reminders). |
| **Share Life Note** | Your referral link, how many friends have joined, and **Gift Life Note**. |
| **Privacy** | **Privacy Lock**: a PIN for this computer. |
| **Theme** | The look of Life Note on the web. |
| **Community** | Our Discord and Instagram. |
| **Billing** | Your plan, your payment method, and canceling or resuming. |
| **Data Management** | **Import**, **Export**, and **Connect Life Note to your AI**. |
| **Account Management** | Your name and email, and **Delete Account**. |

Each section has its own address, so you can bookmark one. **mylifenote.ai/settings#notifications** opens Notifications directly.

### Keyboard shortcuts

Press **?** on any page while you are not typing, or click **Keyboard Shortcuts** under the Settings section list, to see every shortcut. **Ctrl + A** (**⌘ + A** on a Mac) opens Theme.
