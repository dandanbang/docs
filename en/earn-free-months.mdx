Life Note spreads the way a good book does — one person telling another it helped. If you have been that person for someone, we would rather pay you than an advertiser.

## How it works

<Steps>
  <Step title="Find your link">
    Go to **Settings → Share Life Note** and copy **Your referral link**. On your phone, tap **Share Life Note** in Settings. It is yours permanently — the same link every time.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Send it to someone who'd use it">
    They sign up through your link and start journaling.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Watch it count">
    Settings shows how many friends have joined, and your free time is applied once you reach a tier.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  Friends have to actually sign up and use Life Note for a referral to count. This is not a numbers game, and it is not meant to be — five people who genuinely journal is worth more to us than five hundred sign-ups who never return.
</Note>

## Give it as a gift

Someone you'd rather give it to outright? **Gift Life Note** sits right under your referral link, on your phone and on the web. It opens [mylifenote.ai/gift](https://www.mylifenote.ai/gift).

<Steps>
  <Step title="Choose their gift">
    **One month** or **One year** of Life Note Wise.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Make it personal">
    Add their name, yours, and a message if you like.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose when it arrives">
    They get a letter and a gift code by email at the time you pick. They open it and activate it on Life Note.
  </Step>
</Steps>

A gift is prepaid. Nothing renews automatically.

## Who to send it to

The people it lands with are usually the ones already circling the idea — someone who keeps saying they should write things down, someone in the middle of a hard year, someone who reads. It is a poor fit for a mass send, and a very good fit for one honest message to one person.
