## Why journaling?

Research shows that journaling can improve mental health, increase goal achievement, reduce anxiety, and boost memory retention.

Writing makes thoughts tangible, engages the prefrontal cortex, and reduces amygdala reactivity.

Even just 15 minutes per session, 3–4 times a week, can produce lasting benefits.

But writing on a blank page without feedback is hard to sustain.

That’s why Life Note reimagines journaling—not as solitary writing, but as a conversation across time and space.

Read more benefits of journaling [here](https://blog.mylifenote.ai/benefits-of-journaling/).

<Tip>
  American writer Anaïs Nin once said: “We write to taste life twice: once in the moment, and once in retrospect.” Born in 1903, she kept journals for 60 years, and those diaries later became her best-known published works. Writing candidly in a first-person female voice, she laid bare a wide range of inner experiences and has since been regarded as a pioneer of feminist writing.
</Tip>

## Writing your first entry

Start with what's on your mind. A frustration. A question. A moment that stuck with you. A dream you're chasing.

There's no wrong way to begin. Just dump everything that's on your mind. Don't worry about grammar. Don't worry about being profound. Just write.

Research shows that around **150 words and 3 times a week** tends to be the sweet spot—enough depth for meaningful reflection, but not so much that it becomes a chore.

<Tip>
  Discover Campbell Walker’s [VOMIT journaling method](https://blog.mylifenote.ai/the-vomit-system-a-proven-journaling-framework-by-campbell-walker/) — a practical 5-step framework (Vent, Obligations, Mindset, Ideation, Trajectory) to declutter your mind, boost creativity, and stay on track.
</Tip>

Typing is one way in. It is not always the easiest — some days the thought arrives faster than you can type it, some days a photograph says it better, and some days it is already written, on paper.

### Speak it

Tap **Voice** in the composer and talk. Life Note transcribes what you said straight into your entry, where you can edit it like anything else. Pause and resume as much as you like; each recording can run up to five minutes.

It is the fastest way to journal when you are walking, driving, or simply too tired to type — and it tends to be more honest, because you don't get to edit yourself mid-sentence.

<Note>
  Your audio is sent for transcription and then deleted. Only the transcript is kept, and it is yours alone.
</Note>

### Add a photo

Tap **Add Photo** to attach an image to an entry — the view from a trip, a person you wrote about, a page you want to remember. Photos live inside the entry itself and appear in your timeline alongside the words.

### Scan a paper page

If you keep a paper journal, you don't have to retype it. In the mobile app, use **Scan Journal** to photograph a page with your camera or pick one from your library. Life Note extracts the handwriting into text you can edit and save as a normal entry.

## The Wisdom Council

After you are done writing, just hit save. Life Note chooses your **Wisdom Council** for you.

The Wisdom Council reads your entry and matches you with the **most qualified mentors** whose perspectives are most relevant to what you've written. You will also see the qualifications and the reasons why the mentors are recommended to you.

You can also **choose your own** mentors. By going to the mentors tab and following the mind, the mentors will appear in your dropdown in the future.

Once you are ready, just tap **save**.

And if you want to go deeper after reading the responses? Then you can take the conversation further by chatting with the mentors.

## Listen instead of reading

Every mentor response can be heard aloud. Tap the **play** button on a response and your mentor reads it to you.

A small player appears and stays with you — skip back fifteen seconds, forward fifteen, pause, or stop. It keeps playing as you move around the app, so you can listen to a long reply while you scroll your timeline or start writing the next entry.

It works on mentor responses and inside Deep Chat.

<Info>
  Listening is available in English, 繁體中文, 简体中文, 日本語, Español, Français, Italiano, Português, हिन्दी and Українська.
</Info>

<Tip>
  Some replies land differently when they are spoken rather than read — especially the ones you would otherwise skim. Try it on a response you have been avoiding.
</Tip>
